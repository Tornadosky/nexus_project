# Rollout videos: state-only vs state+RGB

Generated by `tools/make_state_plus_rgb_videos.py` from the saved policies in `~/runs_spr/` (no retraining). Every arm is a 250-step greedy rollout in a 1-env vision environment.

## What the frames show

The 64x64 grayscale image is the MuJoCo-Warp camera render.

* For **state+RGB** arms that image is the actor's input, and the caption says whether that seed's actor actually depended on it (measured by the pixel ablation, not assumed).
* For **state-only** arms the actor has no camera pathway at all. The image is a scene view only, and every frame says so. Do not read it as something the policy is looking at.

## What these videos do NOT claim

* A single 250-step rollout is an illustration, not a measurement. The numbers to cite are in `../figures/README.md`, which aggregates 5 evaluation episodes per seed.
* Side-by-side files pair one seed of each arm. Where seeds disagree (WalkerWalk) the pairing is a choice, not a summary -- both walker pairings are provided.
